About HyperLens

HyperLens surfaces Hyperliquid perp trader intelligence:

  • Market — live from the public info API (no key).
  • Leaderboard — the full official leaderboard (~41.7K wallets, ≥$100K acct / ≥$10M vol) from stats-data.hyperliquid.xyz.
  • Wallet Details — top-100 enriched with trading style (HFT/MM · Scalper · Day · Swing · Position) and hold-time stats.
  • Consensus — aggregate positioning of the top-100 traders per coin, with freshness (BUILDING / MIXED / STALE).
  • Watchlist — sustained-best traders that pass the quality filter.

Live sections fetch Hyperliquid directly from your browser. Consensus / wallet details / watchlist are generated daily by the Hermes pipeline and served from data/. Signal-only — not financial advice.
